# Shop Ledger

Shop Ledger is a digital ledger management application designed for small businesses that regularly deal with customer debt and credit transactions. It helps shop owners maintain customer accounts, record transactions, track outstanding balances, and manage traditional *khata* records digitally.

## Overview

Many small businesses operate on a credit and debt basis, making it difficult to manually track customer balances. Shop Ledger provides a simple and organized solution for recording credit and payment transactions and maintaining an up-to-date record of each customer's account.

## Key Features

* Add and manage customer accounts
* Record credit and payment transactions
* Maintain a complete digital *khata* for each customer
* Automatically calculate outstanding balances
* View detailed customer transaction history
* Track total receivables and pending dues
* Identify customers with outstanding payments
* Send WhatsApp notifications to customers regarding their outstanding dues
* Provide a clear and organized overview of business credit records

## Purpose

The purpose of Shop Ledger is to simplify debt and credit management for small businesses by replacing traditional paper-based *khata* systems with a digital, organized, and accessible solution.

## Future Development

Future improvements may include automated payment reminders, real-time data synchronization, user access controls, financial reports, data backup, analytics, and enhanced customer communication.
